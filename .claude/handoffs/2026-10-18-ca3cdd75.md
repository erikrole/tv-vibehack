## Goal
Polish the TV launcher app in `erikrole/tv-vibehack` and keep it on draft PR #1 (https://github.com/erikrole/tv-vibehack/pull/1). Erik reviews the builds on the Basement TV (Hisense).

## Decisions (and why)
- **Install the PR build on the TV:** Erik asked for it. The TV now runs the earlier PR build.
- **Calmer springs:** Erik said the motion was too much. Tile overshoot went from 1.6 to 0.5, tilt from 7° to 3°, and the glass highlight is heavily damped. Control Center eases in instead of bouncing.
- **Remove the hero banner icons:** Erik said they were redundant.
- **Network icon follows the connection:** LAN icon for Ethernet, Wi-Fi icon for Wi-Fi, struck-through when offline.
- **Keep Outfit, sentence case:** Apple's SF font can't be bundled. Wide-tracked ALL-CAPS labels were removed.
- **Sports card layout:** three lines, with a countdown chip.
- **Sleep Timer stays in the TV menu:** the Hisense Menu key opens it, so the launcher never sees it.
