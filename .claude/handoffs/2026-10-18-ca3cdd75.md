## Goal
Fix two launcher problems on the Hisense TV: the focus outline sliding below its icon on a Down press, and choppy animation. Project channel: chan_019eHsDxp1Y4eBM23wy7Ey2eSo6xdC7bRgeAC2a7GNN5eQ. Erik is the reviewer.

## Decisions (and why)
- **Outline:** While the grid scrolls, the outline snaps to its tile every frame. The spring chased a moving target, so it trailed. Sideways moves keep the spring.
- **Benchmark:** Treated "benchmark pass" as launcher performance, since the repo defines no benchmark. Sent Erik the results and asked him to correct this if wrong.
- **Jank:** Full-screen blurred backdrop and glow were the cause. Replaced them with one small opaque 96×54 image scaled up. Dropped the shine sweep and stopped rebuilding the dock gradient each frame. Kept the focus lift and tilt, since disabling them changed nothing.
